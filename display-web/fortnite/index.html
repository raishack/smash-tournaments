Scoring and qualification rules

1.º: 10 points · 2.º: 6 · 3.º: 4 · kill: 1 · eliminating the VIP: 5 extra.

All games in the round count. Points reset to zero in the next round. Tiebreakers: more firsts, seconds, thirds, kills, VIP eliminations, then the lowest random seat number assigned at the start of the round.

Groups are balanced and qualification quotas are fixed before play. Large events have intermediate rounds. Each game has one additional external VIP, without an entrant seat or ranking (up to 100 participants + VIP). Podium places apply only to tournament participants; eliminating the VIP counts as one kill and 5 extra points.
